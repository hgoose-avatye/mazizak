# Fidget development references and status

The original 50-item catalog and research plan remain unchanged as future candidates.
The current approved scope is keyboards 10 + fidgets 10. IDs and shapes here follow the
latest user request, not the older candidate numbering.

All new visuals are procedural development prototypes. Their silhouettes and material
contrasts are functional approximations, not final-quality scans or verified commercial
product reproductions. No product photographs are redistributed.

- L01: https://momoslimes.com/products/fresh-cheese-cream — creator describes thick,
  chewy yellow clay/butter slime that becomes pillowy. Our thick stretchable mound is provisional.
- L02: https://luxenderslimes.com/product/whipped-mango-pancakes-slime/ — creator's
  searchable description specifies thick glossy mango TNG slime. Our glossy spreading
  mound is not a reconstruction of the decorative pancake product. Page retrieval limited.
- L03: https://www.puttyegg.com/pages/slime-texture-guide — maker describes mousse/whipped
  cream textures. https://squeezymagicslime.com/products/milk-slime/ provides white butter
  stretch/fold reference; it is not proof of the exact Korean toy's formulation.
- S01/S02/S03: https://squishyandco.fr/en/blogs/actualites/differences-squishy-mochi-maltose
  distinguishes TPR jelly, PU slow-rise and mochi styles. Specific branded shapes are unverified.
- W01/W02: https://github.com/apple77771/wakppuball-simulator — existing simulator is
  an implementation reference, not a real-product verification source. Thin apple shell
  and thick chocolate chunks follow the user's functional brief, with provisional styling.
- C01/C02: https://thelibraryofslime.com/pages/slime-dictionary and
  https://sloomooslime.com/pages/texture-glossary provide maker texture descriptions.
  Our embedded soap pieces and sugar-bead cluster are provisional structural designs.

Mechanics are bounded real-time approximations: analytic spring/foam relaxation,
local dent fields, volume-redistributing bulges, directional stretch/necking, persistent
fold state, per-face fracture and instanced particles. No finite-element simulation,
true self-colliding folding, free fluid flow or volumetric topology reconstruction is claimed.

Audio: app/public/sfx/wax/CREDITS.md. All sources are CC0 or CC BY 4.0; recordings are
processed into gesture-specific foley. Exact slime, bead, soap and foam-rise recordings
are missing, and are not reported as acquired. Playback is machine-verified only.
